# 📋 Safety Guidelines for Trucker Carriage Platform

## Overview

Safety is our top priority. All users must agree to and follow these guidelines before their first trip. This document outlines the mandatory safety protocols that protect both truckers and customers.

## 🎥 Video Verification Protocol

### Why Video Verification?

Video verification creates a **Digital Chain of Custody** that:
- Protects truckers from false damage claims
- Assures customers their items are handled properly
- Provides evidence in case of disputes
- Builds trust through transparency

### Mandatory Videos

#### At Pickup (Start of Trip)

**1. Trucker's Loading Video** ✅
- **Who**: The trucker
- **What to film**:
  - Wide shot of the luggage before loading
  - Close-ups of all sides showing current condition
  - Any pre-existing damage or marks
  - The loading process
  - Luggage secured in the truck
- **Duration**: 30-60 seconds minimum
- **When**: Before departure from pickup location

**2. Customer's Verification Video** ✅
- **Who**: The customer (or sender)
- **What to film**:
  - Trucker's face and ID (driver's license or platform ID)
  - Vehicle license plate number (clearly visible)
  - Luggage inside the truck
  - Trucker's confirmation statement
- **Duration**: 30-45 seconds minimum
- **When**: Before trucker departs

**3. Witness Video** (Optional but Recommended)
- **Who**: A third party at pickup location
- **What to film**:
  - The entire handoff process
  - Both parties present
  - Confirmation both parties agree
- **Duration**: 20-30 seconds

#### At Drop-off (End of Trip)

**4. Trucker's Delivery Video** ✅
- **Who**: The trucker
- **What to film**:
  - Luggage being unloaded
  - Condition of luggage (all sides)
  - Any damage that occurred during transit
  - Receiver's face
- **Duration**: 30-60 seconds minimum
- **When**: Upon arrival at drop-off location

**5. Receiver's Confirmation Video** ✅
- **Who**: The customer (or receiver)
- **What to film**:
  - Luggage received
  - Inspection of condition
  - Confirmation of satisfactory delivery OR documentation of damage
  - Clear statement: "I confirm receipt of this luggage"
- **Duration**: 30-45 seconds minimum
- **When**: Before trucker leaves

**6. Witness Video** (Optional but Recommended)
- **Who**: A third party at drop-off location
- **What to film**:
  - Delivery handoff
  - Both parties present and satisfied
- **Duration**: 20-30 seconds

### Video Requirements

- **Format**: MP4, MOV, or AVI
- **Quality**: Minimum 720p resolution
- **Audio**: Must be enabled to capture verbal confirmations
- **Lighting**: Adequate lighting to see details clearly
- **Storage**: Videos stored securely for 90 days minimum
- **Privacy**: Videos are only viewable by involved parties and admins in case of disputes

### What Happens If Videos Aren't Uploaded?

- ⚠️ Trip cannot progress to "In Transit" without pickup videos
- ⚠️ Trip cannot be marked as "Delivered" without drop-off videos
- ⚠️ Escrow payment will NOT be released without complete video set
- ⚠️ Repeated violations may result in account suspension

## 🚛 Trucker Safety Guidelines

### Before Accepting a Trip

✅ **Verify Route Compatibility**
- Ensure luggage pickup/dropoff is along your planned route
- Check if detour time is acceptable (max 30 minutes recommended)

✅ **Check Luggage Details**
- Review weight and dimensions
- Ensure you have adequate space
- Confirm cargo doesn't violate regulations (no hazardous materials)

✅ **Verify Insurance Coverage**
- Maintain valid commercial vehicle insurance
- Ensure coverage includes goods transportation
- Update insurance documents in your profile

✅ **Vehicle Inspection**
- Ensure cargo area is clean and dry
- Check securing equipment (straps, bungee cords)
- Verify vehicle is roadworthy

### During the Trip

✅ **Communication**
- Respond to customer messages within 2 hours
- Update customers if delays occur
- Share estimated arrival time

✅ **Secure Storage**
- Keep luggage in locked cargo area
- Use padding for fragile items
- Separate luggage from other cargo if possible

✅ **GPS Tracking**
- Keep location services enabled
- Allow app to run in background
- Update location every 15 minutes minimum

✅ **Professional Conduct**
- Be punctual for pickup and delivery
- Handle luggage with care
- Maintain respectful communication

### Red Flags - Don't Accept If:

🚫 Customer asks to transport illegal items
🚫 Luggage appears suspicious or tampered
🚫 Customer refuses to provide proper contact info
🚫 Payment terms seem unclear or suspicious
🚫 Customer pressures you to skip video verification

## 📦 Customer Safety Guidelines

### Before Creating a Trip

✅ **Prepare Your Luggage**
- Use sturdy, lockable containers
- Label clearly with your contact info (inside and outside)
- Take photos of valuable items before packing
- Consider insurance for high-value items

✅ **Pack Properly**
- Use padding for fragile items
- Seal boxes securely with tape
- Don't overpack - stay within declared weight
- Remove old shipping labels

✅ **Prohibited Items**
Never ship:
🚫 Weapons or ammunition
🚫 Illegal drugs or substances
🚫 Explosives or flammables
🚫 Perishable food items
🚫 Live animals
🚫 Hazardous materials
🚫 Cash or bearer bonds
🚫 Irreplaceable items (family heirlooms, original documents)

### Selecting a Trucker

✅ **Check Credentials**
- ⭐ Rating: 4.0 stars or higher recommended
- ✅ Verified ID badge
- ✅ Background check completed
- ✅ Insurance verified
- 📊 Completed trips: 5+ recommended

✅ **Review Profile**
- Vehicle type and capacity
- License expiration date
- Insurance expiration date
- Previous customer reviews

### During the Trip

✅ **Stay Available**
- Keep phone accessible
- Respond to trucker messages promptly
- Monitor GPS tracking

✅ **Arrival Coordination**
- Confirm receiver availability at drop-off
- Provide clear instructions for delivery location
- Share phone number of receiver

### Red Flags - Cancel Trip If:

🚫 Trucker doesn't show proper ID at pickup
🚫 Trucker refuses to do verification videos
🚫 Vehicle condition seems unsafe
🚫 Trucker appears intoxicated or impaired
🚫 Trucker asks for payment outside the app
🚫 GPS tracking stops for extended periods

## 💰 Payment and Escrow

### How It Works

1. **Customer** pays upfront when trip is matched
2. **Escrow** holds the payment securely
3. **Payment releases** only when:
   - All verification videos are uploaded
   - Trip is marked as "Delivered"
   - No disputes are filed within 24 hours

### Pricing Guidelines

- **Base Rate**: Calculated by distance and weight
- **Platform Fee**: 15% to Trucker Carriage
- **Trucker Earnings**: 85% of trip cost
- **Tips**: Optional, 100% goes to trucker

### Refund Policy

**Full Refund If**:
- Trucker cancels before pickup
- Trucker doesn't arrive within agreed timeframe
- Item is lost during transit (with proper video evidence)

**Partial Refund If**:
- Item is damaged (compensation based on declared value)
- Delivery is significantly delayed

**No Refund If**:
- Customer cancels after pickup
- Damage was pre-existing (shown in pickup videos)
- Customer provided wrong delivery address

## ⚖️ Dispute Resolution

### Filing a Dispute

You can file a dispute within **48 hours** of delivery if:
- Item was damaged during transit
- Item was not delivered
- Wrong item was delivered
- Trucker was unprofessional or unsafe

### Dispute Process

1. **File Report**: Submit dispute through app with evidence
2. **Review**: Admin reviews all videos and messages
3. **Investigation**: Both parties provide statements
4. **Resolution**: Decision made within 5 business days
5. **Action**: Refund, partial refund, or claim denied

### Evidence Required

- All verification videos
- Photos of damage
- Chat message history
- Any additional documentation
